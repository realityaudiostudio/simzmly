A Word About SIMZ

Nurturing Musical Skills with Trinity Syllabus Standards

SIMZ Academy is mainly focused on teaching keyboard classes based on the Trinity College London (TCL) syllabus from 2016 onwards.

SIMZ Academy is an online as well as an offline platform for learning Keyboard and Piano and offers the best musical experience for all candidates. We believe music education should combine rigorous technical mastery with the pure joy of musical expression.

Trinity College London

Structured grades, sight-reading, ear training and international exam prep.

Hybrid Flexibility

Individual Mentoring

Audio Production

Exclusive Sound Engineering recorded masterclasses for music creators.
