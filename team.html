Founder & CEO

Simjo V George

Lead Instructor & Academy Director

Trinity College London Certification:

Secured and awarded Grade 5 in Keyboard and Grade 2 in Piano from Trinity College, London (TCL).

Simjo established SIMZ Academy in 2016 with a singular purpose: to offer students in Chungathara, Manimooly, and remote learners worldwide the highest caliber of keyboard and musical training based on Trinity College London's acclaimed syllabus.

With over a decade of dedicated teaching experience and more than 100 successful students mentored, Simjo combines classical rigor, sight-reading discipline, and ear training with an encouraging atmosphere where every candidate learns to genuinely enjoy making music.

Our Educational Pillars

Why Students Thrive at SIMZ

Our teaching pedagogy is built around verified educational standards and individualized mentoring.

International TCL Benchmark

Lessons follow the prestigious Trinity College London syllabus, preparing students systematically for international certifications and grade exams.

Personalized Pacing

Every student learns differently. We customize instruction speed according to each candidate's finger dexterity, sight-reading comfort, and musical goals.

Modern Studio Infrastructure

Equipped with professional digital pianos, multi-tier synthesizers, and audio monitoring systems across our Chungathara and Manimooly campuses.

Joy-Driven Instruction

"Learning Music means Enjoying the Music." We keep candidates motivated through expressive song playing alongside disciplined technical drills.
